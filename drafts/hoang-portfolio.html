Haziverse

Turns a word list, article, PDF, photo or YouTube link into ready-to-study flashcards with a spaced-repetition schedule.

Live

Desktop app · Writing coach

Inkwell

A Windows app that scores IELTS essays, shows each error against the original and walks you through the rewrite.

v1.9.2 shipped
Problem
  • Writing practice meant jumping between a scorer, a grammar checker and a notes file.
  • Feedback was scattered, so the same mistakes came back.
Turning point
  • Put scoring, an error diff and guided rewriting in one window.
  • Filters by error type: grammar, collocation, spelling.
  • Task 1 charts open beside the essay during review.
Result
  • IELTS Writing 7.5 in my first official test.
  • Packaged installer with a one-click updater; 39 automated checks pass.

Hardware · Embedded

Wi-Fi RC car

A car driven from a phone browser. The board hosts its own Wi-Fi network and control page, no app needed.

In progress
  1. GRADE 11First try with ESP8266 + L298. Code would not upload; I stopped.
  2. OCT 2026Restarted on a NodeMCU board with built-in USB.
  3. 22 OCTFirst drive: forward, back and turns from the phone.
  4. 26 OCTWheel-by-wheel tests to trace a drift to one side.
  5. NEXTFix drift, add ultrasonic obstacle avoidance and a route camera.
Problem
  • Hardware gives slow feedback, and I am used to software's fast loop.
  • An upload kept failing with no error to explain it.
Turning point
  • Found the cause: a charge-only USB cable with no data wires.
  • Wired motors through the L298 and served a control page over the board's own Wi-Fi.
Result
  • Drives on command and stops by itself if the phone disconnects.
  • First physical project I kept at until it worked.

About

From the fence to the factory floor

As a kid I passed the gates of Chu Van An High School and believed it was out of reach. Years later I walked in as a robotics club member, and it felt ordinary. What had changed was me.

I want to design production systems that waste less time, energy and material.

Taiwan builds much of the world's hardware, which is why I am applying there for mechatronics and smart manufacturing. After graduating I plan to work in factory automation for a few years, then lead engineering teams.
